Mall Navigation Prototypes — CS 6750 Team Project

Three medium-fidelity, clickable wireframes of a phone interface for finding the best route through a mall, for single and multiple stops across floors. All three use the same fictional mall, Riverside Commons (3 floors, 17 places).

Prototype A · Idea #41

Reserve-ahead stops

Search for an item, see which stores have it in stock, reserve it, and pick it up with a code at each stop on your route.

OpenOpen with facilitator panel
Prototype B · Idea #47

Shopping progress tracker

Build a stop list, then mark each stop Done or Skip as you go, with a progress bar, time left, and undo or add back for skipped stops.

OpenOpen with facilitator panel
Prototype C · Idea #40

Floor-aware stop ordering

Stops are grouped by floor to cut down on floor changes, with a floor change card between groups and a comparison against the shortest walk.

Facilitator mode. Add ?facilitator=1 to a prototype's address, use the "Open with facilitator panel" links, click the small ≡ button in the bottom-right corner, or press Shift+F. The panel records the participant ID, the task, a timer, every tap inside the phone, screens visited, back presses, facilitator marks, notes, and post-task ratings. Data stays in this browser's local storage and is never sent anywhere. Use the export buttons to download CSV or JSON.
